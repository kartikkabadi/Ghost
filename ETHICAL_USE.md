# Ethical Use Policy

Ghost is an autonomous OSINT research harness. It is intended to help analysts, security researchers, journalists, and developers gather, verify, and synthesize information from publicly available sources.

## Acceptable Use

You may use Ghost to:
- Research publicly available information about organizations, products, or trends.
- Verify claims and detect contradictions across open sources.
- Build defensive security tooling and audit trails.
- Educate and experiment with multi-agent research architectures.

## Prohibited Use

You may not use Ghost to:
- Harass, stalk, dox, or surveil individuals without lawful authorization.
- Gather information for identity theft, fraud, or extortion.
- Violate the terms of service of third-party providers (e.g., search, crawl, or LLM APIs).
- Process sensitive personal data without a lawful basis.
- Conduct any activity that violates applicable local, national, or international laws.

## Design Safeguards

Ghost is built with the following safeguards in mind:
- Multi-source verification before claims are promoted to reports.
- Explicit tracking of blind spots, contradictions, and confidence scores.
- Structured, auditable research outputs.
- No hardcoded credentials or unauthorized access mechanisms.

## Disclaimer

This software is provided as-is for legitimate research and defensive purposes. The authors are not responsible for misuse by third parties.

If you are unsure whether a use case is ethical or lawful, do not use Ghost for that purpose.
